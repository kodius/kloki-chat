Delete your Kloki Chat account

Kloki Chat is a team chat app published on Google Play and the App Store by Kodius.

Request account deletion

Email hello@kodius.com from the email address on your Kloki Chat account, with the subject "Kloki Chat account deletion".

We delete your account, profile, messages and files within 30 days.

Delete individual messages

In Kloki Chat, long-press a message you sent and choose Delete message.
