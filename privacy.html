Kloki Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Kloki Chat is a team chat app published on Google Play and the App Store by Kodius. Kloki Chat is built on the open-source Zulip app and connects to a Zulip server run for your organization.

What data Kloki Chat handles

  • Account information: your name, email address and user ID.
  • Messages, images, videos and files you send or receive.
  • A device token, used only to deliver push notifications.

This data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is used only to run the app. Kloki Chat has no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your organization's Zulip server. For servers hosted on Zulip Cloud, the Zulip privacy policy also applies.
